## Executive Summary

Intelligence gathered from a December 2025 reconnaissance visit to The Lodge at Torrey Pines, combined with extensive web research, suggests the property may be preparing for a **Marriott management takeover**—following the same model as The Resort at Pelican Hill.

Key findings:
1. **General Manager Jakub Skyvara** was brought in by the Evans family specifically to "clean house"
2. **Skyvara left Pelican Hill immediately after its July 2024 Marriott transition**—suggesting he specializes in preparing properties for brand handoffs
3. **Live music has been eliminated** from the property as of December 2025
4. **If Marriott takes over, live music will likely RETURN** as a brand standard requirement

For Pacific Flow Entertainment, this represents both a short-term threat (current "clean house" period) and a significant long-term opportunity (Marriott properties feature live music as a signature element).

## Part 3: How Marriott Management Agreements Work

### Ownership vs. Management

| Aspect | Owner (Evans Hotels) | Manager (Marriott) |
|--------|---------------------|-------------------|
| Property ownership | ✓ Retained | ✗ |
| Day-to-day operations | ✗ | ✓ Takes over |
| Brand standards | Must comply | ✓ Sets standards |
| Staff employment | Transferred to Marriott | ✓ Absorbs with tenure |
| Vendor contracts | Subject to review | ✓ May rebid |
| Capital investment | ✓ Funds renovations | Advises on requirements |

### Likely Brand Positioning for The Lodge

| Brand | Likelihood | Reasoning |
|-------|------------|-----------|
| **The Luxury Collection** | HIGH | AAA Five Diamond, independent character, similar to peer properties |
| **Autograph Collection** | MEDIUM | More flexible standards, preserves independence |
| **St. Regis / St. Regis Estates** | LOWER | Typically larger resort properties; Pelican Hill already claimed for Estates |

### What Would Change

**Branding**: "The Lodge at Torrey Pines, A Luxury Collection Resort" (name preserved with suffix)

**Operations**:
- Marriott Bonvoy integration (loyalty program)
- Brand service standards implemented
- Signature brand rituals (afternoon tea, evening wine hour, etc.)
- Staff training to Marriott specifications
- Vendor contract review and potential rebidding

**What Stays**:
- Property name and identity
- Architectural character
- Local partnerships (likely)
- Key staff (absorbed with tenure guarantees, per Pelican Hill model)
