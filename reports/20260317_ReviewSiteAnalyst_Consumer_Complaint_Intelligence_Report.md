- **58.3%** of consumers never hear back from a company after contacting support.
- **61.18%** unsatisfied or very unsatisfied with proposed resolutions.

- Worst-rated: Uber Eats, McDonald's, Facebook, Google, AliExpress, Lyft, Groupon.
- Best-rated: Hulu, Netflix, HBO Max, Amazon.
- ([PissedConsumer 2025 Survey](https://help-center.pissedconsumer.com/best-and-worst-customer-service/), [Consumer Trends 2025](https://help-center.pissedconsumer.com/consumer-trends/))

## 5. Macro Trends Summary

1. **Satisfaction is structurally stagnant.** The ACSI national score (76.9) has not improved in nearly a decade. Market concentration and rising switching costs allow companies to extract revenue without improving experience.

2. **Rising costs are the dominant driver of dissatisfaction in 2025.** Utility bills (up 34%), insurance premiums, and airline fees are eroding satisfaction across J.D. Power studies even when operational quality holds steady.

3. **Automation is backfiring.** The shift to chatbots and automated support has created a new class of complaint -- the inability to reach a human. AT&T, Facebook, Google, and UHC are repeat offenders.

4. **Health insurance is the emerging rage leader.** UnitedHealthcare's denial of life-saving treatments and multi-hour phone ordeals generate the most emotionally intense reviews in our dataset, amplified by the CEO assassination incident in late 2024 which surfaced deep public anger at the industry.

5. **Review platforms themselves are losing credibility.** Trustpilot (1.1 stars on Sitejabber), ConsumerAffairs (accused of censorship), and Sitejabber (FTC consent order, Jan 2025) all face their own trust crises, complicating the consumer complaint ecosystem.
