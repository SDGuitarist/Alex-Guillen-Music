## 1. CFPB Consumer Complaint Category Rankings

- **3,187,900 complaints** received in calendar year 2024
- **2.8+ million complaints** received in 2025 (through July 22, 2025)

| Rank | Category | Approximate Volume | YoY Change |

| 7 | Vehicle Loans/Leases | Not disclosed | N/A |

### Top Issues Reported
1. Incorrect information on your credit report (+247% vs. 2-year monthly avg)
2. Improper use of your credit report
3. Problem with credit reporting company's investigation into existing problem
4. Debt collectors attempting to collect a debt not owed
5. Managing a checking or savings account

### Most Complained-About Companies
- **Equifax, Experian, TransUnion** dominate complaint volume (the three major credit bureaus)
- Among non-credit-bureau entities: **JPMorgan Chase**, **Wells Fargo**, **Bank of America**, **Capital One**, **Citibank**
- In 2025, Experian's consumer relief rate fell from ~20% to less than 1%; TransUnion's relief rate was halved by October 2025

## 3. DOT Airline Consumer Complaint Statistics

**Source:** [DOT Air Travel Consumer Reports 2025](https://www.transportation.gov/resources/individuals/aviation-consumer-protection/air-travel-consumer-reports-2025); [U.S. PIRG Plane Truth 2025](https://pirg.org/edfund/resources/plane-truth-2025/)

## 4. ACSI / J.D. Power Customer Satisfaction Scores

### American Customer Satisfaction Index (ACSI)

**Source:** [ACSI Q4 2025 Press Release](https://theacsi.org/news-and-resources/press-releases/2026/02/10/press-release-national-acsi-q4-2025/)

- **National ACSI Score: 76.9 / 100** (Q4 2025) — unchanged for two consecutive quarters, down 0.5% annually
- The score has not materially improved since 2017 — roughly the same level as 12 years ago
- Gap between best and worst companies narrowed from 13 points (84 vs. 71) to 7 points (81 vs. 74)

**Notable Low Performers (2025-2026):**
- **NFL fan satisfaction**: Scores lower than any industry measured by ACSI
- **Coinbase**: Debuted at ACSI score of **69** (Finance Study 2026)
- **Credit unions**: Declined 1% to **78**, falling behind banks
- **Super regional banks**: Dropped 3% to **77**

**Macro Warning:** ACSI founder Claes Fornell warns of "pent-up customer defection" — customer switching is artificially suppressed by high switching costs, market concentration, and tariffs discouraging international competition.

### J.D. Power Satisfaction Scores (2025-2026)

| Industry | Score (1,000-pt scale) | Trend |
|----------|----------------------|-------|
| Electric Utilities (Residential) | **499** | **Lowest ever recorded** |
| Utility Digital Experience | 611 | Poor; 32% of utility sites fail basic standards |
| Auto Insurance | 644 | Down 2 pts; 38% of customers in lowest satisfaction segment |
| Home Appliances | 699 | **Down 11 pts** from 710 in 2024 |
| Personal Loans | 704 | Flat (+2 pts); 47% of customers financially vulnerable |
| Auto Dealer Service (EVs) | -51 to -57 pts vs ICE | Wide gap persists |

**Key Driver — Electric Utility Decline:** Average monthly residential electric bill reached **$189** (full year 2025) and **$206** in Q4 2025 — the highest ever measured. Bills have surged **34% since 2020**. Business utility customers: **74%** experienced outages in 2025, with **25%** reporting financial losses.

## 6. Year-over-Year Trend Analysis

### Complaint Volumes: Sharply Increasing Across All Federal Agencies

| Metric | Previous Period | Current Period | Change |
|--------|----------------|----------------|--------|
| CFPB total complaints | ~1.6M (est. 2023) | 3.19M (2024) | ~+100% |
| CFPB complaints Jan-May (YoY) | 2024 baseline | 2025 | **+97.6%** |
| FTC Sentinel reports | 5.4M (2023) | 6.47M (2024) | **+19.8%** |
| FTC fraud losses | $10.0B (2023) | $12.5B (2024) | **+25%** |
| DOT airline complaints | 2023 baseline | 2024 | **+9%** (record high) |
| Mass. AG complaints | Previous year | 2025 | Record high |
| ACSI National Score | 77.4 (2024) | 76.9 (Q4 2025) | **-0.5%** |

### Key Trends

**1. Credit Reporting Complaints Are Exploding**
- CFPB credit reporting complaints up 182% vs. 2-year average
- "Incorrect information on your report" complaints up 247%
- Credit bureau relief rates are collapsing in 2025 (Experian: from ~20% to <1%)

**2. Fraud Losses Are Accelerating**
- Total fraud losses jumped 25% to $12.5B, driven not by more reports but by a higher percentage of victims losing money (27% to 38%)
- Investment scams lead at $5.7B (+24%)
- Cryptocurrency and bank transfers are now the dominant fraud payment methods

**3. Consumer Satisfaction Is Stagnant or Declining**
- ACSI national score has not meaningfully improved since 2017
- Electric utility satisfaction hit an all-time low (J.D. Power score: 499/1,000)
- Home appliance satisfaction dropped 11 points in one year
- The gap between best and worst companies is narrowing — not because the worst improved, but because the best declined

**4. Airline Service Is Deteriorating**
- Record complaint volumes, declining on-time performance (77.9%)
- Frontier's complaint ratio (23.3 per 100K) is 15x worse than Southwest (1.5)

**5. Institutional Consumer Protection Is Weakening**
- CFPB enforcement actions dropped or dismissed under new administration
- Credit bureau accountability declining as relief rates plummet
- DOT OIG found unverified airline penalty data totaling $111M+ in reductions

**6. State AGs Report Record Volumes**
- Multiple states (MA, MO, NY) reporting record or near-record complaint volumes
- Emerging categories: algorithmic/surveillance pricing, online privacy, AI-related complaints

## Data Gaps and Limitations

1. **2026 data**: No federal agency has published 2026 complaint data yet (data typically published with a 3-12 month lag)
2. **CFPB 2025 full-year report**: Not yet published; the most recent annual report covers CY2024
3. **FTC 2025 Sentinel Data Book**: Not yet published
4. **FCC**: Does not publish aggregate complaint volume statistics in an easily accessible annual format
5. **CFPB operational status**: The agency's enforcement and oversight capacity has been significantly reduced under the current administration, which may affect data quality and complaint resolution going forward
